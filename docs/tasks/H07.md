## 4. Two independent death signals are combined

The original documentation explicitly combines:

### Signal A — MapID 87
`MapID == 87 → set respawn_event (xử lý hồi sinh)`

Map 87 is the death/Địa-phủ state used by this Farm module.

### Signal B — real HP 0%
`HP == 0% (giá trị số thật, không phải lỗi đọc) → click (792, 441) hồi sinh 1 lần`

The HP branch requires a genuine numeric 0%, not an unreadable/None value.

Thus an HP-read failure is not death.

## 5. Exact respawn click = one client click at (792,441)

Exact monitor log:

`[HP Monitor] hwnd=<...> HP 0% → click (792, 441) hồi sinh`

The exact doc says:

`click (792, 441) hồi sinh 1 lần`

Therefore:
- click target = client coordinate **(792,441)**;
- the monitor sends **one** respawn click for one latched HP-zero episode;
- no monitor-level click-until-success retry loop is recovered.

This is deliberately different from repeatedly clicking every 4 seconds while HP remains zero.

## 6. hp_latched prevents repeated respawn clicks

Exact original documentation:

`Dùng hp_latched: chặn click lặp khi HP 0% kéo dài, reset khi HP khác 0.`

Therefore:
1. first real HP 0% episode may issue the respawn click;
2. `hp_latched` becomes armed;
3. repeated 0% readings do not issue more respawn clicks;
4. the latch re-arms only after HP becomes non-zero.

This prevents click spam during a prolonged death frame.

A practical consequence is also locked: if the single click is ineffective while HP remains continuously 0, the monitor itself does not spam retries. No hidden repeated-click policy should be invented.

## 7. detected latch prevents repeated MapID-87 recovery events

Exact original documentation:

`Dùng latch detected: set respawn_event 1 lần khi phát hiện 87, chỉ clear khi thoát khỏi 87 — tránh loop vô tận khi di chuyển rời Địa phủ mất vài giây.`

The monitor block directly contains:
- `detected`
- `respawn_event`
- `clear`.

Therefore MapID 87 behaves as a latched recovery state:
1. first detection of 87 sets the recovery event once;
2. repeated 87 reads do not create repeated recovery starts;
3. the event/latch is only re-armed after the account has left map 87.

This is what prevents the Farm cycle from continuously re-entering recovery while movement out of Địa phủ takes several seconds.

## 8. respawn_event is state-based, not click-success-based

The HP-zero click and MapID-87 event are separate paths.

This gives the frozen design an important safety property:
- an HP-zero observation can click respawn;
- the actual Farm recovery event is anchored to the game later reporting **MapID 87**;
- MapID 87 may also be detected even if the HP-zero sample was missed.

Therefore recovery is not considered ready merely because `click_at(792,441)` returned.

The observable map state drives `respawn_event`.
