## 1. Extraction method

Three evidence classes are combined:

1. **Exact Nuitka code-object marker** — printable strings matching `<module NAME>` in the original EXE. These are HIGH confidence.
2. **Physical `.pyd` package file** — extension modules present in the frozen distribution. These are HIGH confidence.
3. **Exact module-like `.py` filename reference** — source-filename strings embedded in the original EXE that do not also have a matching code-object marker. These are MEDIUM confidence references; D01 does not overclaim that every one was executed/loaded at runtime.

Three obvious string artifacts were rejected rather than counted as modules.

All 265 are MEDIUM because D01 evidence for this category is the exact `.py` source-filename strings, not exact Nuitka `<module ...>` markers.

## 7. Native extensions
