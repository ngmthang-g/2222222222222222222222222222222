## 6. B02 correction

B02 previously stated that every captured selected tab showed a dotted focus rectangle. The Start/Xếp-lưới capture disproves that: `▶` is selected while focus is visibly on the `Xếp lưới` radio.

- white/merged tab geometry indicates selection;
