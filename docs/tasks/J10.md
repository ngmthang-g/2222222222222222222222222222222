`Tắt → dừng worker ngay (worker quét cuối untick trước khi thoát).`

Exact worker documentation repeats:

`tắt giữa chừng là thoát ngay (quét cuối untick trước khi thoát)`.

The nested cleanup function is exactly:

The final-sweep log counts:

`acc Nga My`.

Therefore when the checkbox is unticked during an active worker session:
1. stop/exit is requested;

3. those accounts are forced to the OFF pair:
   - `IsAttackMonsterInList=False`
   - `AttackMonsterList=""`;
4. then the worker exits.

Do not copy J09's “stop enforcement only” behavior into J10.
